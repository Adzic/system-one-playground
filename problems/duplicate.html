PROBLEM 1 · INFERENCE

Duplicate the Data

You fit a simple regression, \(y = \beta x + \text{noise}\), with centered data and no intercept. Then someone duplicates every row in the dataset, so \(n\) becomes \(2n\), and refits the identical model on the doubled data. What happens to the estimate, its standard error, the \(t\) statistic, and \(R^2\)?

Beat 3 · The mechanism

With no intercept, the OLS estimate is a ratio of two sums:

\[\hat{\beta} = \frac{\sum xy}{\sum x^2}\]

Duplicate every row and both sums exactly double. The ratio does not move, so \(\hat{\beta}\) is unchanged. The residuals are the same residuals, twice, so SSE doubles; SST doubles too; and since \(R^2 = 1 - \mathrm{SSE}/\mathrm{SST}\), \(R^2\) is unchanged. The fit statistics only see proportions, and duplication preserves every proportion.

The standard error is where the lie enters:

\[\operatorname{SE}(\hat{\beta})^2 = \frac{\hat{\sigma}^2}{\sum x^2}\]

\(\hat{\sigma}^2\) barely changes (SSE doubles, but the degrees of freedom nearly double too: the exact factor for \(\hat{\sigma}^2\) is \(\frac{2(n-1)}{2n-1}\), a hair under 1). But \(\sum x^2\) fully doubles, so \(\operatorname{SE}^2\) halves and SE shrinks by about \(\sqrt{2}\), exactly \(\sqrt{\frac{n-1}{2n-1}}\) for one duplication. Same \(\hat{\beta}\), smaller SE, so \(t\) grows by about \(\sqrt{2}\) with every photocopy.

Why is that wrong? The formula for \(\hat{\sigma}^2\) and SE assumes the errors are independent. A duplicated row's error is not independent of the original; it is the same error, correlated with itself at exactly 1. You have \(n\) pieces of information wearing a \(2n\) costume, and the formula counts costumes.

Beat 4 · At work

Nobody duplicates a dataset on purpose. It happens anyway. The classic is a many-to-many join that silently doubles rows, and suddenly every effect in the model is "significant." Repeated measures get the same treatment when the same person, store, or member contributes many rows that get analyzed as if independent. Member-day tables are the industrial version: one member with 200 event rows is one member, not 200 observations, and standard errors computed at the row level flatter every model built on them.

The fixes match the cause. Deduplicate when the copies are an accident. When the repetition is real, keep the rows but cluster the standard errors on the unit that repeats, the member, the patient, the store. Clustering makes the math count units instead of rows, and the \(\sqrt{2}\) mirage disappears.
